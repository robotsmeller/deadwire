title=Deadwire: Perimeter Trip Lines & Electric Fencing
description=Perimeter alarms and defences you build out of what you scavenged, from tin cans on fishing line up to an electrified fence.
description=
description=[b]Tin can trip line[/b] - three empty cans on a length of line. Single use, and loud enough to pull the street towards it.
description=[b]Reinforced trip line[/b] - reusable, and it knocks over what walks into it.
description=[b]Bell trip line[/b] - the loudest alarm in the mod, heard sixty tiles out.
description=[b]Tanglefoot[/b] - a silent trap. No noise at all, it just puts things on the ground.
description=[b]Electrified deadwire[/b] - the quietest line of the lot, because it is meant not to be found. Needs power from the grid or a generator. With the power off it still works as a plain trip line.
description=[b]Electrified fencing[/b] - wire up a fence you already built. A pasture fence is supposed to be seen: this one is permanent, visible, and bites whatever leans on it.
description=
description=Wires sit on a tile edge and fire when something crosses them, not when something walks alongside. They can be camouflaged, and uncovered again. A destroyed wire leaves its parts on the ground.
description=
description=Server-authoritative throughout, so it is built for multiplayer rather than adapted to it. Forty-two sandbox options on their own settings page. Any power mod that energises a square the normal way works with the electric tier without needing to know about this mod.
description=
description=Requires Build 42.15 or newer.
